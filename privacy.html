喵喵即時口譯 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日

一句話說明：喵喵即時口譯不收集、不上傳、不分享你的個人資料。語音辨識、翻譯與文字辨識都在你的手機上完成；你選擇保留的字幕紀錄只存在你自己的手機裡。

1. 我們不收集資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有我們自己的伺服器，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析、追蹤或當機回報工具。我們（小盜工作室）看不到你在 App 裡的任何內容，App 也不會把你的語音、文字或照片傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。

2. 資料存在哪裡

App 可以把辨識出的原文與翻譯存成「歷史紀錄」。這些紀錄只存在你的手機上，不會上傳。

  • 你可以在 App 的「設定」關閉「儲存歷史紀錄」，也可以隨時按「清除全部歷史紀錄」。
  • 歷史紀錄預設開啟，並有大小上限（預設 20 MB，最高 100 MB）；超過上限時會自動刪除最舊的紀錄。
  • 歷史紀錄是以未加密的文字檔存在 App 的資料夾內。如果你和別人開會，紀錄可能包含其他人說的話；請在需要時關閉儲存或清除紀錄。
  • App 不會錄下或儲存聲音，也不會儲存相機拍到的畫面。
  • 刪除 App 會一併刪除這些紀錄。如果你開啟了 iCloud 或電腦備份，紀錄可能包含在 Apple 的裝置備份中，由 Apple 依照你的設定處理。

3. App 會要求的權限

  • 麥克風：收聽說話聲音，即時轉成字幕。聲音只用來辨識，不錄下、不儲存、不上傳。
  • 語音辨識：把聽到的英文、中文、日文或韓文轉成文字。App 要求辨識在你的手機上進行，不使用雲端辨識。
  • 相機：辨識畫面上的文字並翻譯。文字辨識在手機上完成，不儲存照片。

這些權限都可以隨時到裝置的「設定」裡關閉。App 也可以在你切到其他 App 後，繼續收音最多 5 分鐘，之後會自動停止；這段時間 iOS 會在狀態列顯示麥克風已啟用。

4. 語言資料與翻譯

翻譯使用 Apple 內建的翻譯功能，日文與韓文辨識（iOS 26 以上）使用 Apple 內建的語音辨識。第一次使用某個語言時，iOS 會向 Apple 下載該語言的資料，這個下載由 Apple 的系統處理，本 App 不會傳送你的語音或文字。下載完成後，辨識與翻譯都在手機上執行。

5. 第三方服務

本 App 沒有內建第三方 SDK。語音辨識、翻譯與文字辨識使用 Apple 提供的系統功能，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。

6. 準確度聲明

辨識與翻譯結果可能有誤，也可能漏字，僅供溝通參考，不適合作為法律、醫療或其他需要精確內容的依據。字幕前的「A／B／？」是依聲音音高的粗略判斷，可能標錯，不代表正確辨識說話者。

7. 兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

8. 政策更新

如果之後的版本改變了資料處理方式，我們會先更新這一頁，並在 App 更新說明裡告知。

Privacy Policy for 喵喵即時口譯

Effective October 6, 2026

In short: The app does not collect, upload or share your personal data. Speech recognition, translation and text recognition run on your phone. Caption history, if you keep it, is stored only on your phone.

1. We don't collect data

The app has no accounts, no servers of ours, and no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We (小盜 Studio) cannot see anything you do in the app, and the app does not send your speech, text or photos to us or to any third party.

2. Where your data lives

The app can save recognized text and its translation as "History". History stays on your phone and is never uploaded.

  • You can turn off "Save history" in Settings and clear all history at any time.
  • History is on by default with a size cap (20 MB by default, up to 100 MB); when the cap is reached the oldest entries are deleted automatically.
  • History is stored as an unencrypted text file inside the app's storage. If you are in a meeting it may include what other people said; turn saving off or clear history when needed.
  • The app does not record or store audio, and does not store camera images.
  • Deleting the app deletes this history. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it may be included in your Apple device backup, handled by Apple according to your settings.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: listens to speech to show live captions. Audio is used only for recognition and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Speech Recognition: converts English, Chinese, Japanese or Korean speech to text. The app requires recognition to run on your phone and does not use cloud recognition.
  • Camera: recognizes text in the camera view so it can be translated. Text recognition runs on your phone and no photos are stored.

You can turn these off at any time in Settings. After you switch to another app, the app can keep listening for up to 5 minutes and then stops automatically; iOS shows the microphone indicator in the status bar during that time.

4. Language data and translation

Translation uses Apple's built-in translation feature, and Japanese and Korean recognition (iOS 26 or later) uses Apple's built-in speech recognition. The first time you use a language, iOS downloads that language's data from Apple. That download is handled by the system; the app does not send your speech or text. Once downloaded, recognition and translation run on your phone.

5. Third-party services

The app includes no third-party SDKs. Speech recognition, translation and text recognition use system features from Apple, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

6. Accuracy

Recognition and translation can be wrong or miss words and are meant as a communication aid only, not as a basis for legal, medical or other work that needs exact content. The "A/B/?" label before a caption is a rough guess based on voice pitch and may be wrong; it does not reliably identify who is speaking.

7. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the release notes.
